## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** An intimate performance after midnight: a Chinese spirit welcomes a Western Halloween guest and turns an ordinary bad day into a warm, personal identity.
- **OWN-WORLD:** Near-black stage, warm ivory lettering, pumpkin footlights, a real freestanding character. Self-hosted Bodoni Moda for Latin display, Ma Shan Zheng for Chinese display; quiet system text for instructions.
- **STORY:** Enter the theatre → tell an absurd story → meet the cast → leave with an identity and invite a friend. Native scroll, continuous surfaces, spacious type and a cast list; no icon-feature boxes or fake social proof.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Theatre name, readable invitation, actual Meng Po GLB occupying the stage, direct encounter action and visible language choice. Understandable without animation or WebGL.
- **FORM:** GSAP ScrollTrigger changes stage orientation and light as the story passes it. Framer Motion owns interactive button feedback only. CSS sticky anchors the stage; no scroll hijacking. Mobile has an inline stage and smaller spatial movements.
- **FINISH:** Keyboard focus, reduced-motion static composition, poster/error recovery, no autoplay microphone/audio, 3D loading state, real-asset and route-parameter checks. Seven voice characters; only Meng Po currently has a 3D model.
