# 黄泉议会 / Nether Council

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, pnpm workspace, React Three Fiber / Three.js, Qwen realtime audio and text, Hunyuan GLB, Vercel Node Functions with WebSocket upgrades. No database in the first release.

## Users
AI, developer and founder community party participants and their invited friends; Chinese and English speakers. First a ten-person pilot, then fifty people.

## Product Purpose
Turn a ridiculous everyday story into a warm, funny encounter with a Halloween character and a personal identity card worth sharing.

## Positioning
A Chinese mythology × western Halloween party experience. A public microphone spectacle and a separate personal mobile journey share the same characters.

## Operating Context
Mac browsers and a shared microphone / venue speakers on the main screen. QR entry on phones. Noisy, dim party rooms; participants can interrupt the character while it speaks.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Seven roles, starting with one real Meng Po duplex voice and one real generated GLB. Chinese or English speech with bilingual captions. Identity card PNG and QR referral. Bound sessions and server-side credentials. No placeholder voice or model may count as real acceptance evidence.

## Brand Commitments
Gentle teasing of the event, a warm ending, identities people want to claim. Meng Po serves pumpkin forget-your-worries soup; Zhong Kui handles party security; Hei and Bai Wuchang host the night. Frankenstein's Monster, Werewolf and Vampire join the roster. Understandable to English speakers without knowing Chinese mythology.

## Evidence on Hand
Confirmed interview decisions and specs/001-halloween-party/{spec,plan,tasks}. Product and implementation acceptance have not yet passed real device or participant trials.

## Product Principles
Prove one complete real journey before scaling assets. Speak naturally with barge-in. Derive the verdict from the participant's story. Share by choice. Measure completed conversations and referred conversations, not just page views.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable captions, keyboard access, reduced motion, visible error recovery, explicit microphone gesture. Never promise browser echo cancellation works on untested venue equipment.
